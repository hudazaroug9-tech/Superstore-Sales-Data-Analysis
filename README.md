# Superstore Sales & Performance Analytics

## Project Overview
An end-to-end Data Analysis project analyzing retail sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, and regional shipping efficiency using **Python** for data cleaning/EDA and **Power BI** for business intelligence dashboarding.

## Executive Summary
This enterprise retail dataset consists of **9,800 records** covering sales operations across four years. The analysis reveals a total revenue generation of **$2.26M** across **4,922 unique orders** placed by **793 customers**, yielding an **Average Order Value (AOV) of $459.48** and an average fulfillment window of **3.96 days (~4 days)**.

---

## Key Business Insights

* **Revenue Acceleration:** Strong revenue growth trajectory over the 4-year period, with peak revenue momentum achieved in **2018**.
* **Category Performance:** **Technology** leads total sales revenue at **$827.4K**, followed closely by **Furniture** ($728.6K) and **Office Supplies** ($705.4K).
* **Customer Retention:** A high ratio of orders (~4.9K) relative to unique customers (793) demonstrates strong repeat purchasing behavior.
* **Fulfillment SLAs:** Operational shipping times averaged 3.96 days across regions, ensuring reliable delivery cycles.

---

## Strategic Recommendations
* **Targeted Marketing:** Focus promotional efforts and inventory allocation toward high-margin **Technology** products prior to Q3/Q4 demand surges.
* **Inventory Balancing:** Optimizing stock levels in **Furniture** and **Technology** to avoid fulfillment delays during seasonal spikes.
* **Logistics SLA:** Maintain the sub-4-day fulfillment benchmark as a key customer retention metric during high-volume periods.
